Long · ~150 words

Astro Mahri is a rapper, producer and DJ, born and raised in New Orleans and now working out of Durham, North Carolina. The city’s heat and cross-cultural imagination run through the writing: bars that balance raunchy wordplay with honest commentary and magnetic storytelling, with references to your favorite fandoms slipped in along the way. The delivery is as playful as it is precise, as bookish as it is bold.

His debut single “Madness” arrives October 23, 2026, released direct through Bandcamp, SoundCloud and Qobuz rather than the major streaming platforms.

Behind the decks he plays dance parties, raves and festivals, flipping anime themes into club edits and keeping the script moving. The same instinct runs through everything else: he wrote, designed and directed the interactive mystery at the heart of the Future Hooman art gallery, and leads operations and art curation for Lore Con. It is one body of work, not three jobs — building places where the brilliant get wild, the weird get loud, and everybody is a star.
